About BISTI

We Turn Potential Into Employable Skill

Baira International Skills Development & Training Institute (BISTI) is a modern vocational training centre dedicated to producing competent, confident and job-ready technicians. Our workshops, labs and classrooms are equipped to international standards so that every learner practises on the real thing.

  • Hands-on training in fully equipped workshops
  • Nationally recognised skill certificate
  • Assessment & trade testing support
  • Career counselling & overseas documentation help
